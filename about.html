We post on Craigslist every day. Now we do it for you.

Scale Classifieds started as the posting desk inside SSAFF Media, a performance-marketing company that runs hundreds of Craigslist posts a week across 413 US cities for its own brands. The accounts, the phone numbers, the per-city fee table, the people who know which titles get flagged — that operation now takes outside orders.

The deal is simple: you pay what Craigslist charges plus a flat $15 per post, and a person posts your ad and sends you the link. No subscriptions, no software to learn, no bots.
