How it works

You do the part only you can do — the ad. We do the part nobody enjoys — the posting.

1 · Write your ad

  • —Pick the Craigslist section and category.
  • —Title (70 characters), body, up to 8 photos.
  • —The email replies go to, and a phone number if you want one shown.
  • —Optional link with traffic-source masking: a clean per-city link on our domain, referrer stripped, clicks counted per city.
  • —Saved to your account so you can post it again in new cities later.

2 · Pick your cities

  • —Search 413 US metros by name or state, or add the top 10, 25, or 50 in one click.
  • —Choose 1–5 posts per city. Extra posts go up on staggered days.
  • —Post now or pick a start date.
  • —The price updates live as you add cities.

3 · Pay and relax

  • —One card payment through Stripe.
  • —Our team reviews the ad, then posts it from our own accounts in each city.
  • —Every post is live within 24–48 hours. You get an email with every link and a status page that updates as posts go up.
  • —Questions? Message the team from your order page.

After posting

Craigslist ads run for 30 days, then expire. Your status page keeps every link, and you can post the same ad again in one click from your account.

If a post is removed

Craigslist and its users can flag posts without giving a reason. Removed within 7 days of going live? We repost it once at no charge. Honest, specific ads get flagged far less.

What it costs

Craigslist's own fee for that section and city plus $15 per post. Free categories mean $15 flat. Every price is shown before you pay.
