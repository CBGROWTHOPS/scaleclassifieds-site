Questions people ask first

Do you post from my Craigslist account?+

No. Our posting team uses its own accounts and phone-verified numbers. You never share a login, and you never touch Craigslist's checkout — we pay the posting fees and bill you once.

How fast do posts go live?+

Standard turnaround is 24–48 hours from payment, or from the start date you choose. You get an email with every live link and a status page that updates as posts go up.

Is a person posting, or a bot?+

A person. Automated posting is what gets ads flagged and accounts banned. Every post is placed by hand by someone who does this all day.

Can I schedule a start date?+

Yes. Pick "as soon as possible" or a date. Extra posts per city are staggered across days so they don't all land at once.

Where do replies go?+

Craigslist relays replies to the email you put on the ad, or shows your real address if you choose that. If you add a phone number you decide whether calls, texts, or both are okay. Replies never pass through us.
