<h2>Why every blog post about this is wrong</h2>
<p>Three reasons:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Craigslist changes fees without announcing it.</strong> The gigs fee didn&#39;t exist a few years ago. Services went from free to $5. Rental fees were added to Boston and Chicago after New York. Any article older than a year is suspect, including the one you&#39;re reading — which is why we date it.</li>
<li><strong>The per-city numbers aren&#39;t published anywhere official.</strong> Everyone&#39;s list is somebody&#39;s compilation.</li>
<li><strong>The fee is shown at the end of the flow.</strong> Most people quoting fees have posted in two or three cities and are extrapolating.</li>
</ol>
<p>Our approach: the fee table that prices every Scale Classifieds order is maintained by the people posting. When a poster sees a different number at Craigslist&#39;s checkout than our table says, the table gets corrected and marked verified. It&#39;s not perfect on day one; it gets more accurate with every post.</p>
