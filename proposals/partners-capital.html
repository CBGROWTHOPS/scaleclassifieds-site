Posting frequency

Craigslist listings expire after 30 days, but visibility fades long before that. Page one of a category holds 120 results, and every new listing pushes the older ones down.

How quickly that happens is entirely a function of the market. In a dense metro a commercial listing can be off page one inside a day. In a quieter one the same listing holds the front page for weeks. One cadence applied across both overspends in the second and leaves you invisible in the first.

So we measure it rather than guess. Before setting a schedule we check the live turnover in each market your properties sit in — how many new listings go up per day, and how long that leaves a post on page one — and size the frequency to match. Each property gets the cadence its own market needs, and we revisit it once there is a month of your results to work from.
